# Lead & Lead 2K26 — Homepage Improvement Menu

## Current assessment

The website already has a distinctive identity: the cinematic entry, the game-world homepage, the Hall of Banners, the sheet-backed Live Vanguard, the conference logo, and the registration route now form a recognisable Lead & Lead experience. The strongest visual ingredients are the **moonlit navy atmosphere**, **display typography**, **cobalt structural details**, and **copper actions**.

The next opportunity is not simply adding more visual effects. It is making the homepage answer a delegate’s practical questions faster, provide stronger reasons to register, and turn the game-world style into a more continuous conference story. The options below are intentionally separated so you can choose only the additions that fit the event.

> **How to choose:** Reply with the item numbers you want, for example: `1, 3, 6, 9, 10, 19`. You can also write **“all priority items”**, **“visual only”**, or **“remove item 14”**.

## Important guardrails

The following should only be added when backed by real information: testimonials, ratings, attendee counts, confirmed speakers, sold-out messages, capacity claims, venue information, prices, and countdown dates. The website should never display invented social proof or placeholder organiser details as if they were real.

When you reply with your chosen item numbers, I will turn them into an implementation plan and ask only for the missing official information needed to build them accurately.
